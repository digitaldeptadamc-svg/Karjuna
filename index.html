Department of Dravyaguna
Kharjura
Botanical Name :

  • Phoenix dactylifera Linn

    • Family Name :

  • Arecaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Kharjura

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Khajur

  • English – Date Palm

    • Synonyms :

  • Madhura Phala

  • Swaduphala


    • Habitat :

  • Widely grown in Rajasthan, Gujarat, Punjab, and parts of South India, Cultivated in arid and semi-arid regions.


    • External Morphology :

  • Stem: Tall, erect, unbranched palm with a rough trunk covered by old leaf bases.

  • Leaves: Large, pinnate (feather-like), bluish-green leaves forming a crown.

  • Flowers: Small, creamish-yellow, borne in large branched spadices.


    • Useful Parts :

  • Fruit (Phala)


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa –Madhura

  • Guna –Guru, Snigdha

  • Vipaka - Madhura

  • Virya - Sheeta


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Balya

  • Brimhana

  • Vrishya


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Daurbalya (General Weakness): Fruits are consumed as a nourishing tonic.

  • Raktalpata (Anaemia): Fruits are used as a supportive dietary supplement.


    • Dose

  • Phala (Fresh Fruit): 25–50 g

  • Phala Majja (Fruit Pulp): 10–20 g


    • Formulation:

  • Kharjuradi Mantha

  • Kharjura Avaleha

  • Kharjura Churna
